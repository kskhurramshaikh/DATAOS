Built for regulated industries

The same governed pipeline — quality-gated ingestion, entity resolution, a logged decision trail — applies across every sector where data has to be trusted before it's used. A few of the frameworks it's designed around:

These reflect the standards and frameworks DataOS's pipeline is designed around, mapped to what it actually does today — not a claim of third-party certification. Exact scope varies by engagement; ask us for specifics.

Banking & Financial Services

IFRS 9 SDAIA NDG SAMA CSF

Expected credit loss computed to IFRS 9 methodology — staged, scenario-weighted, independently calculated. NDI readiness scoring built around SDAIA's data governance maturity model.

Government & Public Sector

SDAIA NDMO PDPL NCA ECC

The same NDI scorecard used for banking applies directly to public-sector data-maturity assessment. Every governance decision logged with reviewer identity.

Telecom

PDPL CITC

Entity resolution reduces duplicate subscriber records — a core data-accuracy control. Data held at Silver until it clears quality gates.

Retail

PDPL ISO 27001

Duplicate-customer detection with human-in-the-loop review — records are flagged and confirmed, never automatically merged.

Healthcare

PDPL ISO 27001

Personal data held at Silver until it clears quality gates, never promoted silently. Audit trail on every review decision.

Education

PDPL ISO 27001

Student and staff data governed through the same quality-gated pipeline as any other regulated dataset.

Energy

NCA ECC PDPL

Audit trail and reviewer accountability on every governance decision across critical-infrastructure data.
